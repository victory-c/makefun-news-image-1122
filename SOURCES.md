# Sources and editorial decisions

Edition: Sunday, 22 November 2026. Researched in the recorded build session using live web retrieval and search. Facts are frozen in main.py; execution never fetches news. Short summaries are original writing, not reproduced article text. Drawings are original symbolic illustrations.

1. Climate — COP31, Antalya, Turkey
   The Guardian, Fiona Harvey and Graham Readfearn, 22 Nov 2026, first published 07:54 EST, updated 11:14 EST.
   https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/nov/22/cop31-climate-talks-set-to-end-in-deep-division-as-key-deal-rejected
   Countries rejected the overarching text because it omitted fossil fuels. Related decisions passed. The image therefore says the main deal failed, not that the conference achieved nothing. Lead selected for global consequence and a specific Sunday decision.

2. Conflict — Benghazi, Libya
   Al Jazeera, 22 Nov 2026.
   https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/11/22/suspected-drone-strike-hits-russian-vessel-in-libya
   The Guardian, 22 Nov 2026.
   https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/22/blast-russian-ship-suspected-drone-attack-benghazi-libya
   Satellite imagery identifies the Merkury and shows most of it capsized and sunk. No actor immediately claimed responsibility. The image calls it a suspected sea-drone strike, not an established Ukrainian operation; it omits provisional casualty estimates. The Saturday Baltic attack is not presented as Sunday's event.

3. Technology and politics — South Korea
   The Guardian, Angelique Chrisafis and agencies (AFP and Reuters), 22 Nov 2026, first published 00:04 EST.
   https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/22/south-korea-education-minister-resigns-after-ai-cheating-scandal-engulfs-university-exams
   Choi Kyo-jin's resignation is Sunday's action. The AI-assisted tax-accountancy cheating confession and university-exam security response preceded it. The image does not claim widespread proven cheating in the university exam itself.

4. Extreme weather — Tupanciretã, Brazil

   Carlos Alcaraz beat Alexander Zverev 6–2, 7–6, taking the second-set tiebreak 9–7, for his first ATP Finals title. Score formatting is explicit to avoid confusing a tiebreak score with a set score.

Discovery and exclusions

- Consulted The Guardian's dated world, environment and sport indexes, Wikipedia's dated current-events digest and Bing search. Wikipedia served as a lead finder; the final selected stories were checked against the reports above.
- Direct Reuters and AP pages returned access errors. Their names appear only where an accessible article explicitly credits their reporting, not as a claim to have read inaccessible originals.
- Excluded the Mexico arrest: video was published Sunday but the capture occurred on 20 November. Avoided presenting old captures as new events.
- Excluded other earlier-week stories despite prominence in current-news recommendations.
- The image aims for a representative editorial selection, not a complete wire-service digest. The source names and exact date appear in the image because only image bytes reach the judge.

Typography

Glyph coverage was baked from installed DejaVu fonts by tools/prepare_type.py (Pillow is a development-only tool). The resulting coverage data in assets/type.pack is used by the original standard-library compositor. No font-rendering implementation was copied. DejaVu's Bitstream Vera license and attribution are preserved in assets/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt. No news photographs, logos, or third-party illustrations are used.
